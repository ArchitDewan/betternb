# Power rule

## Method

For a constant exponent n, the derivative of x^n is n times x^(n-1). Apply the rule term by term to sums and differences. Constants differentiate to zero.

## Common mistake

Do not reduce the exponent without first multiplying by it. For example, the derivative of x^4 is 4x^3, not x^3.

## Example

If f(x) = 3x^4 - 2x + 7, then f'(x) = 12x^3 - 2.
