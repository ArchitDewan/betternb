# Product rule

## Method

For f(x) = u(x)v(x), use f'(x) = u'(x)v(x) + u(x)v'(x). Both original factors appear once in the result.

## Common mistake

The derivative of a product is not generally u'(x)v'(x). Differentiate one factor at a time while leaving the other unchanged.

## Example

For f(x) = x^2 sin(x), f'(x) = 2x sin(x) + x^2 cos(x).
