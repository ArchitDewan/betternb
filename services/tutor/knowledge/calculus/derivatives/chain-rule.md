# Chain rule

## Method

For a composite function f(x) = g(h(x)), differentiate the outer function at h(x), then multiply by h'(x). Keep the inner expression intact until the final multiplication.

## Common mistake

A frequent error is differentiating the outer function but omitting the derivative of the inner function.

## Example

For f(x) = (x^2 + 1)^3, the outer derivative contributes 3(x^2 + 1)^2 and the inner derivative contributes 2x, giving f'(x) = 6x(x^2 + 1)^2.
